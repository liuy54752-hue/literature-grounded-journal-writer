# Citation Integrity

Use this reference before presenting extracted evidence or a manuscript draft.

## Non-Negotiable Rules

- Do not fabricate authors, years, titles, page numbers, DOIs, findings, methods, samples, or claims.
- Do not cite a source for a claim unless the uploaded material supports that claim.
- Mark missing metadata as "not confirmed in provided materials."
- Mark claims that need user verification instead of filling gaps from memory.
- Do not silently convert original text into paraphrase without preserving the source anchor in working tables.

## Direct Quotation

Use direct quotation only when:

- The wording is unusually precise, canonical, or theoretically important.
- The quote is short.
- The source, author, year, and page or location are available or clearly marked as needing verification.

Avoid long quotation blocks in manuscript drafts unless the user explicitly requests them and citation rules allow it.

## Paraphrase

A paraphrase must:

- Preserve the source's meaning.
- Change sentence structure and wording substantially.
- Keep the citation attached.
- Avoid broadening the claim beyond what the source supports.

## Synthesis

A synthesis claim combines multiple sources. It must make clear whether sources converge, contrast, or create a tension.

Safe synthesis wording:

- "Together, these studies suggest..."
- "Prior work has generally emphasized..."
- "This stream of research leaves open..."
- "While one line of work highlights..., another suggests..."

## Author Inference

Author inference is reasoning created for the target article. It may rely on literature, but it is not a direct source claim.

Signal author inference with wording such as:

- "Building on this logic..."
- "This suggests that..."
- "Extending this argument to the target context..."
- "The present article therefore argues..."

## Final Audit

Before finalizing, check:

- Every in-text citation appears in the reference list if a reference list is provided.
- Every reference-list item cited in text is actually used or marked unused.
- Every quoted excerpt has a source anchor.
- Every unsupported claim is revised, qualified, or marked as needing evidence.
- No generated DOI, page number, author, or year was added from assumption.
